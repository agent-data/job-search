# Job Preferences Brief

**Summary:** Mid-to-senior AI/ML engineer (individual contributor) building production ML and
LLM systems; fully remote within the US; wants real ML work on a product that ships.

## Must-haves / dealbreakers
- Remote within the US. (An on-site-only role is a dealbreaker.)
- Works on AI/ML or LLM-powered products (not ML-in-name-only).
- No crypto/web3: a company or product built on crypto, blockchain, or web3 is a dealbreaker.

## Strong preferences
- Mid-to-senior level; real technical ownership of models or ML infrastructure.
- Python + cloud + modern ML/LLM tooling.
- A product team that ships quickly with low process.

## Nice-to-haves
- A path toward senior/staff scope.
- Meaningful equity.

## Red flags
- "AI" used only as marketing with no real ML work.
- Heavy on-call or ops burden with no engineering time.
